Privacy Notice

Effective date: 2026-08-25. This notice is provided by JohnPrice.

Information related to the app

ShiftLedger is designed for personal shift planning and record keeping. It does not provide a user account, does not operate a cloud service for core records, and does not include advertising or general app-usage analytics. It does not use your information for cross-app or cross-site tracking.

Core records are intended to be kept on your device. A selected export or share leaves the device when you start that action. Operating-system or device backup behavior depends on your platform and settings. Export copies, device backups, and records held by Apple are outside a Clear All action in the app.

Purchases and Apple

If purchase features are available, ShiftLedger communicates directly with the Apple App Store through StoreKit 2 and does not use a third-party purchase aggregator. Apple may process purchase history, subscription status, transaction data, and information required to complete, restore, verify, or manage a purchase. Those purchase-service activities are not general behavioral analytics.

Purchase-service information can be used to present offers, complete a transaction, restore access, and manage an entitlement. Provider communication can occur for these purchase-service activities even when you have not exported or shared a record.

ShiftLedger does not keep Pro access available during a billing grace or billing-retry state. When current paid access cannot be verified, Pro features lock while existing records remain available to read.

Apple's privacy notice is available at Apple Privacy.
